# T-039: Restore Space playback and add a one-second sentence repeat gap

- Status: Complete
- Priority: P1
- Requirements: OVER-001, OVER-004
- Dependencies: T-035, T-037

## Background

After selecting a sentence repeat button, browser focus remains on that button. Space activates the focused button and toggles repeat instead of pausing or resuming the audio. Repeating without a pause also leaves no time for the learner to speak.

## Scope

- Included: Space playback from the focused sentence repeat button; a one-second silent interval between repeats; safe pause, cancel, switch, and material-load behavior during the interval.
- Excluded: Recording or scoring the learner's voice.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Space pauses/resumes audio without toggling sentence repeat when the repeat button has focus; Enter/R still toggle repeat.
- [x] Every sentence repeat cycle has about one second of silence before the same sentence plays again.
- [x] During the silent interval, Space stops the pending replay; another Space resumes deliberately. Switching/cancelling the target or loading audio cancels pending replay.
- [x] E2E tests use isolated audio fixtures and cover button focus, timing, cancellation, and existing shortcuts.

## Implementation

Space now intercepts playback while the focused control is a sentence-repeat button, preventing its browser-default click. A repeat cycle pauses the audio and schedules the same sentence after a 1000 ms timer. Pending timers are cancelled on target change, word seek, back/restart, and material load. Space during the interval cancels the timer and leaves playback stopped; another Space restarts at the sentence beginning. The button shows waiting and paused states.
