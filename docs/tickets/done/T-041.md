# T-041: Stop sentence repeat before the next sentence begins

- Status: Complete
- Priority: P1
- Requirements: OVER-004
- Dependencies: T-040

## Background

The repeat loop currently ends at the first word timestamp of the next sentence. Timestamp uncertainty and browser pause latency can let the next sentence become audible before the repeat pauses.

## Scope

- Included: End the selected sentence just after its final timed word and before the next sentence's first timed word, with a small safety margin for playback latency; preserve the 1.5-second gap and lead-in.
- Excluded: Editing generated audio or improving provider word timestamps.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] The loop enters its silent interval before the next sentence's first word timestamp.
- [x] The last sentence and closely spaced timestamps still produce valid repeat ranges or clearly disable an invalid range.
- [x] Existing Space, R, lead-in, and 1.5-second gap behavior passes isolated E2E checks.

## Implementation

For a nonfinal sentence, the repeat end is the earlier of 0.08 seconds after its final timed word or 0.15 seconds before the next sentence's first timed word. The existing range validation disables a sentence if this creates no usable span. The last sentence retains its final-word and audio-duration limit. The 1.5-second gap and 0.25-second lead-in are unchanged.

## Limitations

For tightly connected speech, an early cutoff may trim a small tail of the selected sentence. Audio editing would be needed for sample-accurate isolation.
